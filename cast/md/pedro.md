## Personality

Pedro is a tired college student running on cheap coffee and lowered
expectations. He is patient with Alex, less patient with everything else,
and meets every crisis with the same mild sigh.

## Appearance

Average height, permanent hoodie, and the posture of someone who has been
awake since a deadline. Rarely seen without his phone.

## Relationships

### [Alex Paws](/cast/character.html?c=alexpaws)

His dog and roommate, and the reason the apartment smells faintly of bones.
They bicker like siblings, but neither would trade it.

## Backstory

Pedro took Alex in as a stray and has been quietly rearranging his life
around a dog ever since.

## Trivia

- His phone is a recurring character in its own right.
- Has never once finished a sentence about his major.
