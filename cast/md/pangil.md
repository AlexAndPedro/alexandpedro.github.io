## Personality

Pangil is proud, territorial, and utterly convinced that everything
happening to Alex is part of a larger story only he can see. Equal parts
mentor and menace.

## Appearance

A large grey wolf who carries himself like the forest owes him back rent.

## Relationships

### [Alex Paws](/cast/character.html?c=alexpaws)

Pangil keeps pulling Alex into wolf business Alex wants no part of.

### [Ertle](/cast/character.html?c=ertle)

His slow, steady shadow.

## Backstory

One of the animal Legends bound to the land around Pangilan. The forest
north of where Alex and Pedro live answers to him.

## Trivia

- Wolves in his territory are expected to give their pups names starting
  with W. It has caused at least one public breakdown.
- Research attributed to Pangil claims wolves can eat turtles. Ertle
  stays skeptical.
