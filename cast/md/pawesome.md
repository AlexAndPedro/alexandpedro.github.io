## Personality

Pawesome is the longtime superdog of Pangilan. Earnest, speech-prone, and
seemingly unaware that most of the city's disasters resolve themselves
right about the time he arrives.

## Appearance

Caped. Heroic jaw. Always lit as though there is a sunset directly behind
him.

## Relationships

### Fans

Pawesome has a devoted fanbase that faints in large numbers whenever he
speaks. He has had to discipline them for looming over bystanders.

## Backstory

Nobody &mdash; the author included &mdash; is quite sure where Pawesome
came from. He has simply always been saving Pangilan.
