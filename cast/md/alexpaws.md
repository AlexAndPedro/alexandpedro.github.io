## Personality

Alex is, by his own firm insistence, a completely ordinary dog. He is
easygoing, a little lazy, and far too interested in human things he has no
business caring about &mdash; the news, chocolate, and why Oreo is good with
milk. He rarely panics, even when the city is ending again.

## Appearance

A standard Labrador retriever: short yellow coat, floppy ears, and a collar
he never takes off. He is most often drawn mid-shrug.

## Relationships

### [Pedro Perez](/cast/character.html?c=pedro)

Alex's owner and roommate. The two share a small apartment and an even
shorter list of life goals. Most of their arguments are about the
thermostat.

### [Ertle](/cast/character.html?c=ertle)

A box turtle Alex meets on the way out of the city. Slow to move, slower to
answer, and a steady friend once you wait for him.

### [Pawesome](/cast/character.html?c=pawesome)

The superdog of Pangilan. Alex is thoroughly unimpressed by the cape.

## Backstory

Alex turned up as a stray and was taken in by Pedro. Whatever happened
before that, he does not talk about.

### Present Day

Alex now splits his time between the apartment and the forest north of the
city, where a wolf legend named Pangil keeps insisting he is part of
something bigger.

## Trivia

- He cannot actually eat chocolate. This has never once stopped him asking.
- His middle name, "Wanter", is never explained.
- First appeared eight strips into the series, in *Finally, Alex!*
