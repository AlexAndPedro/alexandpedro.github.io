## Personality

Ertle is not dumb. He is just slow &mdash; slow to walk, slow to answer,
slow to be bothered by anything. Give him enough time and he usually lands
on the right answer.

## Appearance

A box turtle: small, high-domed shell, in no rush.

## Relationships

### [Pangil](/cast/character.html?c=pangil)

Ertle trails Pangil around the northern forest. Pangil does most of the
talking.

### [Alex Paws](/cast/character.html?c=alexpaws)

Met on Alex's trip out of the city, and stuck around.
