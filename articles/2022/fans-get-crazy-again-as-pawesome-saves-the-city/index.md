<span class="ArticleLocation">PANGILAN CITY, PANGILAN —</span> In a massive Pangilan metro, you could say another world-destroying event is happening every day. Today, it was a massive meteor.

Fortunately, the longtime superdog Pawesome saved the city again. He was in front of Pangilan City Hall to give a speech. 

“Pangilan City is safe once again,”

His fans, as always, were in front of the podium. Just in front of the podium, Pawesome fans were cheering.

“Pawesome! I love you! Give me your fur!”

About five minutes into the speech, fans of Pawesome collapsed on the ground. The medical team expected this and brought out the stretchers that were bought with funds from the Pawesome Collateral Damage Tax which increased income tax by 60%.

“It appears that the fans were fainting due to getting too excited to see Pawesome. Fortunately, some of the hyperventilating fans were saved thanks to the limited edition Pawesome Air Bag™ that deployed after the device felt a sudden drop in height.”

About 120 fans collapsed.

After the speech, the remaining two people who got lost finding the supermarket were forced to clap as Pawesome’s fans loom over them. When Pawesome noticed this incident, he had to discipline his fans.

“Hey, you bad bad fans. That’s not how you treat other people.”

The fans prostrated themselves and cried for forgiveness.

“Almighty Pawesome-sama. We seek your forgiveness. Also, is this the time of the year when dogs shed their fur?”

Pawesome left the podium as soon as he heard another incident from far away. Riots broke out around the city hall, demanding for his return.
