<span class="ArticleLocation">WOLOT, PANGILAN —</span> Local wolf Woronton Wilnor caused a disturbance at Wolot Animal Hospital Wednesday morning after discovering that nearly every acceptable W-name for his newborn pup had already been claimed. Increasingly unhinged, Woronton demanded a book of wolf names.

> "Wardor is taken. Winston is taken. Wolpin is taken. I don't know what other names are available."

He claimed that the websites that can give him wolf name suggestions were not helpful.

> "Who even names their pups Wankadon? That sounds weird."

A few minutes later, a wolf bystander approached Woronton and suggested a name that does not start with the letter W. The suggestion was immediately rejected and the unnamed wolf became *lupus non gratus* in Woronton's territory.
